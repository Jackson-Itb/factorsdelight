## Description

A Farmer's Delight addon for packing a harvest and selling it.

The **Factor** is a villager who buys crates and bags from **Farmer's Delight** and **Crate Delight** and pays emeralds. Grow the crops, pack them, and trade the crates. One staple crate (9 crops) pays 1 emerald, about two and a half to three times what a farmer pays for loose crops. Each offer has a limited number of sales and restocks up to twice a day, so a hall of Factors is how a factory scales.

Craft a **Factor's Counter** from paper, sticks, a barrel, and three straw. Place it where an unemployed villager can reach it. One counter employs one villager. They also need a bed.

**Requires** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.219 or newer, Farmer's Delight 1.3.4 or newer, and Crate Delight. Create is optional: mechanical crafters follow the normal crate recipes. Expanded Delight is optional. Peanut crates, salt bags, and cinnamon bags are bought only when it is installed.

This mod is still in development. Prices and trades may change.
